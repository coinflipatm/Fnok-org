The name

Yeah, it means

it means.

Not a slur. Not a joke about anybody. A promise: you’re f’n ok, exactly as He made you.

We could have picked a name that sits quietly in a church lobby. The people this is for have never been allowed to sit quietly anywhere. So the name is loud and the message is simple.

If the word bothers you more than the way some people get treated, sit with that a minute.

The story

Wear it to church.
Wear it to the cookout.

FNOK started in Flint, Michigan, with a man of God who’s been judged plenty and figures you have too. He read the same Book everybody else did and kept landing on the parts about loving people, not sorting them.

Every shirt is pulled by hand, one at a time, by the guy who came up with it. No warehouse. No drop calendar. Just a press, some ink, and a point to make.

Wear it around somebody who needs to read it.
